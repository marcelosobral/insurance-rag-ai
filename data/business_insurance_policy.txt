PREMISES LIABILITY COVERAGE:
Coverage for bodily injury and property damage occurring on your business premises:
- Slip and fall incidents involving customers or visitors
- Injuries caused by defective conditions on premises
- Property damage to visitor belongings
- Liability for independent contractors working on premises

PRODUCTS LIABILITY COVERAGE:
Coverage for injury or damage caused by products you manufacture, sell, or distribute:
- Software defects causing financial loss to clients
- Errors in consulting services provided
- Failure of products to perform as warranted
- Product recall expenses (with endorsement)

COMPLETED OPERATIONS LIABILITY:
Coverage for work performed away from your premises after completion:
- IT system installations that later cause problems
- Software implementations that fail after project completion
- Consulting recommendations that prove inadequate
- Training services that lead to operational issues

PERSONAL AND ADVERTISING INJURY:
Coverage for non-physical injuries including:
- False arrest, detention, or imprisonment claims
- Malicious prosecution allegations
- Wrongful eviction or entry claims
- Libel, slander, or defamation lawsuits
- Copyright, trademark, or patent infringement claims
- Invasion of privacy or misappropriation of advertising ideas

EXCLUSIONS:
This policy does not cover:
- Professional liability (requires separate E&O policy)
- Employment practices liability (discrimination, harassment)
- Cyber liability and data breach incidents
- Intentional criminal acts
- War, terrorism, and nuclear incidents
- Pollution and environmental cleanup
- Aircraft, auto, or watercraft liability (separate policies required)
- Workers compensation (separate policy required)
- Damage to your own work or products

ADDITIONAL COVERAGES INCLUDED:
- Legal defense costs (unlimited, outside policy limits)
- Supplementary payments for investigation costs
- Medical payments for immediate medical expenses
- Prejudgment interest on judgments
- Post-judgment interest and appeal bonds
- Cost of bail bonds (up to $250)
