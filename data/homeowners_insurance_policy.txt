COVERED PERILS FOR DWELLING AND OTHER STRUCTURES:
This policy covers risks of direct physical loss except those specifically excluded:
- Fire and lightning
- Windstorm and hail damage  
- Explosion and smoke damage
- Vandalism and malicious mischief
- Theft and attempted theft
- Damage from aircraft or vehicles
- Riot and civil commotion
- Falling objects (trees, aircraft parts)
- Weight of ice, snow, or sleet
- Accidental water damage from plumbing systems
- Sudden electrical damage to appliances

PERSONAL PROPERTY COVERAGE:
Covered for risks listed above plus additional perils. Special limits apply to:
- Money, coins, precious metals: $500 total
- Securities, accounts, deeds: $1,500 total
- Jewelry, watches, furs: $2,500 total
- Firearms: $2,500 total
- Silverware, goldware: $2,500 total
- Business property: $2,500 on premises, $500 away from premises
- Computer equipment: $5,000 total
- Credit cards, electronic fund transfers: $500 total

MAJOR EXCLUSIONS:
This policy does not cover loss caused by:
- Flood (separate flood insurance required)
- Earthquake and earth movement
- War, nuclear hazard, government seizure
- Intentional loss caused by insured
- Business activities (separate business insurance needed)
- Motor vehicles, aircraft, watercraft (except limited coverage)
- Ordinance or law requiring rebuilding to current codes
- Normal wear and tear, deterioration
- Pollution and contamination
- Mold, fungus, wet rot, dry rot (unless from covered peril)

LIABILITY COVERAGE:
Personal liability coverage protects against claims for:
- Bodily injury to others on or away from your property
- Property damage caused by you, family members, or pets
- Legal defense costs and court judgments
- Volunteer work activities (excluding professional services)

Coverage applies worldwide but excludes:
- Business and professional activities
- Intentional injury or damage
- Motor vehicle, aircraft, or watercraft liability
- Property in your care, custody, or control

ADDITIONAL LIVING EXPENSES:
If your home becomes uninhabitable due to a covered loss:
- Pays for temporary housing (hotel, rental home)
- Additional costs for meals if unable to cook at home
- Storage costs for personal property
- Pet boarding if temporary housing doesn't allow pets
- Coverage continues until home is repaired or 24 months, whichever comes first

SPECIAL FEATURES:
- Replacement cost coverage for dwelling (no depreciation)
- Guaranteed replacement cost up to 125% of Coverage A limit
- Personal property replacement cost (items over $500)
- Inflation guard automatically increases coverage by 4% annually
- Home office coverage up to $2,500 for business equipment
- Identity theft coverage up to $25,000 for restoration expenses
- Service line coverage for buried utilities up to $10,000

LOSS SETTLEMENT:
- Dwelling and other structures: Replacement cost (no depreciation)
- Personal property: Replacement cost for items over $500, actual cash value for items under $500
- Must actually repair or replace property to receive replacement cost settlement
- If cost to repair exceeds policy limit, actual cash value applies

CLAIM REPORTING:
Report claims immediately by calling 1-800-HOMECLAIM or online at www.homeinsurer.com. Protect property from further damage and keep receipts for temporary repairs. Insurance company will arrange for property inspection within 48 hours of claim report.
