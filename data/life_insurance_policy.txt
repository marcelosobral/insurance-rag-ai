GRACE PERIOD AND LAPSE:
- 31-day grace period for premium payments
- Policy lapses if premium not paid by end of grace period
- Automatic premium loan provision if sufficient cash value exists
- Reinstatement possible within 5 years with evidence of insurability

INCONTESTABILITY CLAUSE:
This policy becomes incontestable after 2 years from issue date, except for:
- Non-payment of premiums
- Fraud in the application
- Suicide clause (2-year exclusion from issue date)

EXCLUSIONS:
Death benefits will not be paid if death results from:
- Suicide within 2 years of issue date (premiums will be refunded)
- Death occurring while committing a felony
- War or act of war (whether declared or undeclared)
- Aviation activities (except as fare-paying passenger on commercial airline)

WAIVER OF PREMIUM RIDER:
Premium waiver included at no additional cost:
- Waives premiums if insured becomes totally disabled before age 65
- Disability must last continuously for 6 months
- Proof of continuing disability required annually
- Cash values continue to accumulate during waiver period

ACCELERATED DEATH BENEFIT RIDER:
Terminal illness benefit included at no additional cost:
- Up to 80% of death benefit payable if diagnosis of terminal illness
- Must have life expectancy of 12 months or less
- Benefit reduces death benefit payable to beneficiaries
- No waiting period required

ASSIGNMENT AND CHANGE OF BENEFICIARY:
- Policy may be assigned as collateral for loans
- Beneficiary may be changed at any time (unless irrevocable designation)
- Changes must be in writing and received by insurance company
- Company not responsible for validity of beneficiary changes

SETTLEMENT OPTIONS FOR BENEFICIARIES:
Death proceeds may be paid as:
- Lump sum payment (default)
- Interest-only payments with principal retained
- Fixed period payments over specified years
- Fixed amount payments until funds exhausted
- Life income payments based on beneficiary's age and sex

POLICY VALUES GUARANTEED:
All guaranteed values are based on mortality table and interest assumptions stated in policy. Current values may be higher based on company experience but are not guaranteed.
